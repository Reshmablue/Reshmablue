Practical Retail Suggestions for Local Kirana

AI Practical Suggestions
💡
Checkout Counter Impulse Zone:

Place high-margin small confectioneries, chocolates, and mints directly adjacent to the billing counter to boost average bill value.

📦
First-In First-Out (FIFO) Storage:

Rotate packaged groceries and personal care products so older stock is sold before newly arrived consignments to eliminate expiry losses.

⚡
Reorder Level Discipline:

Restock items as soon as alerts appear to prevent lost sales on high-demand staples like Rice, Sugar, and Edible Oils.
